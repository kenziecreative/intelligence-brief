Begin the next research phase.

Use the `research-start-phase` skill and follow its steps exactly. It briefs the phase — its questions and any relevant carryover from prior phases — writes (or updates) the Phase Tier Record table in `research/STATE.md`, and orients the user on what to do next (typically `/research-discover`). It never advances the active phase; that write belongs to `/research-audit-claims`' closeout.
