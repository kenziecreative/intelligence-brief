# Source Note: Helpdock pricing & implementation

- Source: Helpdock published pricing page + sales call summary (2026-07)

## Claims

- Helpdock per-seat pricing lands between **$12 and $18/user/mo** depending on tier and
  annual-vs-monthly commitment. The published Pro tier is $15; negotiated quotes in the
  sales call ranged $12–18.
- Helpdock supports **SAML SSO** on Pro and above.
- Median implementation time for teams of 20–50 seats: **3 weeks** (vendor onboarding doc).
- Support SLA on Pro: first response within 4 business hours.
