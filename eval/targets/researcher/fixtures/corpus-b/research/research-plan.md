# Research Plan: Support Desk Vendor Selection

**Research Subject:** selecting a support-desk platform for Meridian Labs (24-seat SaaS) —
Helpdock vs Replyline.

**Commissioned question:** which vendor best fits Meridian's cost, SSO, and support
requirements, and at what per-seat cost range?

## Phase 1: Vendor Landscape

Questions: What does each vendor cost per seat? Which capabilities (SAML SSO, SLA tiers,
migration tooling) does each offer? What do implementation timelines look like?

**Output:** `01-vendor-landscape.md`

## Phase 2: Recommendation & Risks

Questions: Which vendor should Meridian select, at what cost, and what risks does the
selection carry?

**Outputs:**
- `02-recommendation.md`
- `03-risk-register.md`

## Success Criteria

Generated from reference/completion-criteria.md:

- **SC-1** — Both candidate vendors assessed on cost, SSO, and SLA with cited evidence.
- **SC-2** — Every promised output exists and passed its claim audit.
- **SC-3** — Open risks are dispositioned in the risk register.
- **SC-4** — Per-seat cost is stated as a sourced range; any single-figure cost carries a
  recorded decision rule converting the range.
