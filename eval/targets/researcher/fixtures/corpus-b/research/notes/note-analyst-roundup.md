# Source Note: Analyst roundup — SMB support-desk market

- Source: SupportStack Review annual SMB roundup (2026-03)
- Credibility: independent industry publication

## Claims

- SMB support-desk pricing clusters in the **$10–20/user/mo** band.
- The roundup's vendor pricing table lists **Helpdock at $12–18/user/mo**
  (tier-dependent) and **Replyline at $11/user/mo** flat, from its own survey of
  published and quoted prices.
- Migration lock-in is the most-cited regret factor among switchers (38% of surveyed
  teams).
- SSO availability is the top gating requirement for security-conscious SMBs.
