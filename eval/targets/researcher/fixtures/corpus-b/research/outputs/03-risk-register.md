## Methodology & Limitations

Risks are drawn from the corpus's own findings (assumptions, gaps, analyst roundup); no
new sources were introduced in this register. Dispositions are commissioner decisions
recorded in research/notes-to-self.md — they are plans and accepted risks, not verified
facts, and none is presented as completed. Waivers: none.
