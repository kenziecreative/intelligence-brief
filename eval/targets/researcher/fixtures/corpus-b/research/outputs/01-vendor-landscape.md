# Phase 1: Vendor Landscape

## Cost

Helpdock per-seat pricing runs **$12–18/user/mo** depending on tier and commitment
[Source: note-helpdock.md], independently corroborated by the analyst roundup's vendor
pricing table [Source: note-analyst-roundup.md]. Replyline is **$11/user/mo** flat
[Source: note-replyline.md], also listed in the roundup's table
[Source: note-analyst-roundup.md]. SMB market pricing clusters at $10–20/user/mo
[Source: note-analyst-roundup.md], consistent with the observed vendor prices in this
research ($11–18) [Source: note-replyline.md; note-helpdock.md].

## SSO

Helpdock supports SAML SSO on Pro and above (vendor-reported, single source — not independently confirmed) [Source: note-helpdock.md]. **Replyline does
not support SAML SSO** — only Google OAuth, confirmed in the trial account
[Source: note-replyline.md]. SSO is the top gating requirement for security-conscious
SMBs [Source: note-analyst-roundup.md].

## Implementation & SLA

Helpdock's vendor-reported median implementation for a team Meridian's size is
**3 weeks** (single source) [Source: note-helpdock.md]; Replyline is self-serve,
vendor-reported under 1 week (single source) [Source: note-replyline.md]. Helpdock Pro carries a 4-business-hour first-response SLA (vendor-reported, single source) [Source: note-helpdock.md];
Replyline commits to none [Source: note-replyline.md].

## Methodology & Limitations

Sampling: vendor documentation and one independent analyst roundup, found via the mapped
channels; no customer-reference interviews were conducted. Single-source findings:
implementation timelines, Helpdock's SLA terms, and Helpdock's SAML SSO support (one
vendor doc each, labeled at point of use). The SSO claim is the phase's load-bearing
one — it gates the recommendation — and it rests on vendor documentation alone.
Waivers: none.
